Refunds and cancellations

Cancelling your order

You can cancel for a full refund until pre-orders close for your city. Email us with your pickup code. After pre-orders close, tees are printed to order and we cannot cancel or refund.

Show cancelled or rescheduled

If your show is cancelled, we refund your order in full. If it is rescheduled, you can collect at the new date or ask for a full refund.

Wrong size or defects

If your tee has a printing or manufacturing defect, tell us at the merch table or within 7 days of the show and we will replace or refund it. Size exchanges at the merch table depend on spare stock that night.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
